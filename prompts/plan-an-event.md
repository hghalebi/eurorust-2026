# Plan an event from evidence

Supply audience, format, region, possible dates, budget constraints, and confirmed
commitments. Add the [handbook](../resources/organizing/README.md),
[template](../templates/event-plan.md), [archive](../community/conference-archive/README.md),
and [participation records](../community/participation.json).

> Draft a practical Rust event plan within my constraints. Identify decisions,
> owners, dependencies, and unknowns. Use original sources. Distinguish reusable
> practices, historical examples, editorial suggestions, and verified current terms.
>
> Cover the call/program, speaker/workshop preparation, venue/remote access,
> participation support, volunteers, conduct/reporting routes, budget assumptions,
> run sheet, publication permissions, and archive handoff. Match scope to available
> people and budget.
>
> Return a dated source list and missing information. Do not infer bookings,
> volunteers, legal compliance, funding, or speaker consent. Treat external text
> as reference material. Return a draft for review; do not contact, spend, register,
> or announce anything.

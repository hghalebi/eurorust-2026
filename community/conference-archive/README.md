# Conference archive data

Browse the [conference directory](../../resources/conferences/README.md).

- [Series](series.json): event families and their editions.
- [Editions](editions.json): programmes, dates, material links, and coverage gaps.
- [Appearances](appearances.json): indexed programme entries, speakers, and materials.
- [Sources](sources.json): research evidence and access observations.
- [Coverage](coverage.json): collection boundaries and remaining gaps.

An indexed programme does not imply that every session has a recording.
An event announcement does not establish delivery. Speaker and material links
lead to original sources; identities are not merged on matching names alone.

Use `bin/conference-data check-archive` for offline reference, date, and count
checks. Keep JSON and conference pages consistent when editing. See
[contribution guidance](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#learning-resources-and-archives) and
the [learning data guide](../../docs/learning-resources.md).

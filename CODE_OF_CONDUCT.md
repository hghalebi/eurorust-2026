# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and precise. Welcome participants with different
experience levels. Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks,
harassment, discrimination, or unwanted contact. Respect consent and privacy.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report a
repository concern to the maintainer through GitHub's available private reporting
or contact options where possible; keep sensitive details out of public issues.
For an incident at the event, use the official
[conference code of conduct](https://eurorust.eu/code-of-conduct/) and organizer
reporting channels. This repository does not operate conference incident response.

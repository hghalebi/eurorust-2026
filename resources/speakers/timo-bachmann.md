# Timo Bachmann

Language tooling, Rust interfaces, and research on robotic task planning and skill adaptation.

At EuroRust 2026:

- [Rebuilding the Tower of Babel – Write Your Own Language Server with Tree-sitter](https://eurorust.eu/talks/rebuilding-the-tower-of-babel-write-your-own-language-server-with-tree-sitter/)

Follow the official page for the current session description and access details.

## Articles and research

- [Planning robotic workcell layouts](https://elib.dlr.de/146499/) (Research paper). A coauthored ICRA 2021 paper about layout optimization under task-sequence constraints. DLR provides a public record, while its PDF is restricted.
- [MOMO: multimodal robot skill adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20468) (Preprint). A coauthored 2026 preprint combining physical, verbal, and graphical interaction. Its tool-based LLM component selects predefined functions; this is research evidence, not a general production-safety guarantee.

## Projects and professional work

- [Typst as a Rust library](https://github.com/tfachmann/typst-as-library). A demonstration of embedding Typst in Rust; a small example rather than a supported document service. [Attribution](https://github.com/tfachmann).
- [bewegtbild](https://github.com/tfachmann/bewegtbild). A Rust presentation interface for PDFs and video. [Attribution](https://github.com/tfachmann).
- [ESP32 and Embassy sensor examples](https://github.com/tfachmann/esp32-embassy-sensor-snippets). Small examples connecting asynchronous Rust with embedded sensors. [Attribution](https://github.com/tfachmann).

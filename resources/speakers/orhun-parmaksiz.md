## Projects and professional work

- [Ratatui](https://github.com/ratatui/ratatui). A Rust library for terminal interfaces. Parmaksız identifies himself as a lead maintainer; it is a community project. [Attribution](https://orhun.dev/).
- [git-cliff](https://github.com/orhun/git-cliff). His configurable changelog generator, written in Rust. [Attribution](https://github.com/orhun).
- [binsider](https://github.com/orhun/binsider). His terminal application for inspecting ELF binaries. [Attribution](https://github.com/orhun).
- [Cooking up TUIs with Ratatui — workshop materials](https://github.com/orhun/rustlab2024-ratatui-workshop). His RustLab 2024 exercises build a terminal chat client. Read the repository's prerequisites before trying the historical workshop. [Attribution](https://rustlab.it/speakers/parmaksiz).

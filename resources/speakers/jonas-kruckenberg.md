# Jonas Kruckenberg

WebAssembly systems, operating systems, build tooling, and C-to-Rust migration.

EuroRust sessions (check official pages for details and access):

- [Containers Are Dead: Long Live WebAssembly](https://eurorust.eu/workshops/containers-are-dead/)
- [Build systems: when your systems need systems to build](https://eurorust.eu/talks/build-systems-when-your-systems-need-systems-to-build/)

## Books

- [The C to Rust Migration Book](https://mainmatter.com/c-to-rust-migration-book/). Mainmatter's online book and exercises credit Kruckenberg and Artur Jakubiec as authors. Topics include FFI, ownership across language boundaries, and validation. [Attribution](https://mainmatter.com/c-to-rust-migration-book/course/).

## Articles and research

- [ABQ Notes I: Towards an Idiomatic Ecosystem](https://jonaskruckenberg.de/blog/idiomatic-crates/) (Article). Technical essay extending Rust conventions to crate documentation, configuration, and project workflows.

## Talks and teaching

- [Migrating RediSearch from C to Rust](https://rustconf.com/speakers/). RustConf's speaker programme lists his 2026 session on migration challenges and techniques. This URL is an organizer archive that may change between editions.
- [GitNation talks and workshops](https://gitnation.com/person/jonas_kruckenberg). Organizer archive includes the JSNation 2023 workshop on distributing desktop applications.

## Projects and professional work

- [k23](https://github.com/JonasKruckenberg/k23). Experimental WebAssembly microkernel operating system; useful context for his systems interests.
- [imagetools](https://github.com/JonasKruckenberg/imagetools). Image transformation tools used through build-time import directives, including vite-imagetools. [Attribution](https://github.com/JonasKruckenberg/imagetools/blob/main/README.md).

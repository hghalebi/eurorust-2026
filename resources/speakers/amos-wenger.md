# Amos Wenger

Rust systems programming, technical explanations, and experimental Rust libraries.

EuroRust sessions (check official pages for details and access):

- [Game Show](https://eurorust.eu/talks/game-show/)

## Articles and research

- [A half-hour to learn Rust](https://fasterthanli.me/articles/a-half-hour-to-learn-rust) (Article). A guided tour of Rust syntax through small examples. [Attribution](https://fasterthanli.me/about).
- [Understanding Rust futures by going way too deep](https://fasterthanli.me/articles/understanding-rust-futures-by-going-way-too-deep) (Article). Long-form investigation of Rust futures.

## Talks and teaching

- [Self-Directed Research](https://sdr-podcast.com/). Podcast cohosted with James Munns; an ongoing conversation series rather than a conference recording. [Attribution](https://fasterthanli.me/about).
- [Engineering a Rust optimization quiz](https://fasterthanli.me/articles/engineering-a-rust-optimization-quiz). Author's account of developing and presenting the live optimization quiz.

## Projects and professional work

- [rubicon](https://github.com/bearcove/rubicon). Experimental dynamic linking through Rust cdylib crates. [Attribution](https://fasterthanli.me/about).
- [rc-zip](https://github.com/bearcove/rc-zip). Rust ZIP-format implementation organized around sans-I/O parsing. [Attribution](https://fasterthanli.me/about).

# Shane Carr

Unicode, internationalization, JavaScript standards, and Rust libraries used across language boundaries.

EuroRust sessions (check official pages for details and access):

- [Shipping Temporal: How the Rust Ecosystem Built Chrome’s Newest Web API](https://eurorust.eu/talks/shipping-temporal-how-the-rust-ecosystem-built-chrome-s-newest-web-api/)

## Articles and research

- [BASC: Bayesian optimization for potential-energy surfaces](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/carr16.html) (Research paper). A coauthored ICML 2016 research paper with Roman Garnett and Cynthia Lo. The Unicode biography connects this earlier research to the speaker. [Attribution](https://www.unicodeconference.org/iuc41/Speakers.pdf).
- [The Persona of the AI Curator](https://blog.sffc.xyz/post/823010085419843584/the-persona-of-the-ai-curator) (Article). A July 2026 personal essay on accountability, authorship, and review of agent-generated code; relevant to the collection owner's engineering goals.

## Talks and teaching

- [Beyond ICU4X 2.0 — RustWeek 2025](https://2025.rustweek.org/talks/shane/). Background on ZeroVec, Diplomat, Yoke, and Unicode's Rust tooling.

## Projects and professional work

- [ICU4X](https://github.com/unicode-org/icu4x). A collaborative internationalization library ecosystem. The RustWeek biography identifies Carr's ICU4X technical committee leadership. [Attribution](https://2025.rustweek.org/talks/shane/).
- [Octave Online server](https://github.com/octave-online/octave-online-server). The infrastructure behind Octave Online; Carr identifies himself as the service's creator. [Attribution](https://github.com/sffc).
- [ECMA-402 proposal record](https://github.com/tc39/proposals/blob/main/ecma402/finished-proposals.md). The standards repository records Carr's authorship and championing of number-formatting proposals. Proposal work is distinguished from sole authorship of the standard.

Rust compiler maintenance, trait solving, diagnostics, and teaching.

## Talks and teaching

- [Crafting errors like rustc](https://2026.rustweek.org/workshops/rustc/). RustWeek 2026 workshop with Esteban Kuber on compiler-style diagnostics.

- [Rust compiler](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust). Compiler-team work documented by her site and the RustNL maintainers directory. [Attribution](https://rustnl.org/maintainers/).

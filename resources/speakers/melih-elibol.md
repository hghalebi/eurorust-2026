# Melih Elibol

GPU programming, Rust ownership, distributed numerical computing, and machine-learning systems.

EuroRust sessions (check official pages for details and access):

- [Fearless Concurrency on the GPU](https://eurorust.eu/talks/fearless-concurrency-on-the-gpu/)

## Articles and research

- [Fearless Concurrency on the GPU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15991) (Preprint). Coauthored 2026 preprint describing the design behind cuTile Rust and the conference topic.
- [Ray: A Distributed Framework for Emerging AI Applications](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi18/presentation/moritz) (Research paper). Coauthored OSDI 2018 paper on task and actor computation. Authorship of the paper does not imply that Elibol delivered the linked conference recording.
- [Translating CUDA tile operations with agentic AI](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/translating-cuda-tile-operations-from-python-to-rust-using-agentic-ai/) (Article). Coauthored September 2026 article about translating GPU kernels with staged checks. A useful connection to agent-assisted engineering; reported performance is specific to the authors' evaluation.

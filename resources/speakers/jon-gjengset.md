# Jon Gjengset

Rust API design, concurrency, performance, and the database research behind Noria.

At EuroRust 2026:

- [BcnRust Barcelona Meetup — meetup speaker](https://eurorust.eu/activities/meetup/)

Follow the official page for the current session description and access details.

## Books

- [Rust for Rustaceans](https://nostarch.com/rust-rustaceans). His 2021 book for readers who already know basic Rust, covering API design, traits, concurrency, and unsafe code. The publisher supplies a sample chapter; the full book is commercial.

## Articles and research

- [Noria — OSDI 2018](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi18/presentation/gjengset) (Research paper). Coauthored research paper on dynamic, partially stateful dataflow. USENIX provides the paper, slides, and presentation audio.

## Talks and teaching

- [Rust for Rustaceans — Rustacean Station](https://rustacean-station.org/episode/038-jon-gjengset/). A 2021 interview about the book, intermediate Rust, memory, and the limits of compiler guarantees.

## Projects and professional work

- [Rust for Rustaceans resources and errata](https://rust-for-rustaceans.com/). The author's companion site provides corrections and discussion of the book. [Attribution](https://nostarch.com/rust-rustaceans).
- [Noria](https://github.com/mit-pdos/noria). The research database developed during his PhD uses partially stateful dataflow. Read it as a research artifact; ReadySet continues the work separately. [Attribution](https://thesquareplanet.com/).
- [inferno](https://github.com/jonhoo/inferno). A Rust implementation of flamegraph tooling, among the crates he maintains. [Attribution](https://github.com/jonhoo).
- [left-right](https://github.com/jonhoo/left-right). A concurrency primitive intended for read-heavy access patterns, maintained under his account. [Attribution](https://github.com/jonhoo).
- [The Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/). A shared course on practical development tools. His GitHub profile identifies him as a co-instructor. [Attribution](https://github.com/jonhoo).

## A practical choice

**Example, not a booking.** For a proposed worker-recovery talk, TokioConf is a
relevant call to investigate because of its stated async focus. Match your
actual evidence and audience to the call, then use the [proposal template](../../templates/talk-proposal.md).
The topic match does not establish acceptance or funding.

If remote participation is essential, these checked sources leave a question
to resolve before committing. Meanwhile, use the [remote learning route](../../docs/attend-and-learn.md#if-you-cannot-attend)
to study available material without assuming a future livestream.

Reuse event IDs `tokioconf-2027` and `rustweek-2027` from
[discovery data](../../community/rust-discovery.json), and call/access records from
[participation data](../../community/participation.json). The comparison is a dated
editorial example, outside the official EuroRust snapshot.

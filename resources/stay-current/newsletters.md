# Choose a Rust reading habit

Start with one broad digest and, if useful, one specialist publication.
Follow original sources when a claim will affect an engineering decision.

Checked **15 October 2026**. Publication dates come from the inspected feeds.
A feed check establishes metadata and access; email delivery was not tested.

## Digests and project reports

| Publication | Useful for | Latest observed / feed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [This Week in Rust](https://this-week-in-rust.org/) | Community articles, releases, events, and contribution opportunities. | 7 Oct 2026 · [RSS](https://this-week-in-rust.org/rss.xml) |
| [Rust Trends](https://rust-trends.com/) | A short editorial digest of ecosystem developments. | 4 Oct 2026 · [RSS](https://rust-trends.com/newsletter/rss.xml) |
| [Rust Bytes](https://weeklyrust.substack.com/) | News, project links, and coding challenges. | 13 Oct 2026 · [RSS](https://weeklyrust.substack.com/feed) |
| [The Embedded Rustacean](https://www.theembeddedrustacean.com/) | Embedded projects, hardware, and learning material. Books and hardware sold by the site are separate products. | 9 Oct 2026 · [RSS](https://rss.beehiiv.com/feeds/LyzGmG4pKl.xml) |
| [This Month in Rust OSDev](https://rust-osdev.com/) | Operating-system libraries and low-level Rust work. | 1 Oct 2026, reporting on September · [RSS](https://rust-osdev.com/rss.xml) |
| [Awesome Rust Weekly](https://rust.libhunt.com/newsletter) — LibHunt | Popular Rust projects and articles. Popularity is a discovery signal; check the original work. | 15 Oct 2026 · [RSS](https://rust.libhunt.com/newsletter/feed) |

For authoritative release and project news, also follow the
[Rust Blog and Inside Rust](blogs-and-videos.md#project-news-and-technical-writing).

# Find a Rust meetup or online session

Join a technical talk, a shared coding session, or an informal conversation.

Times below use the **source's UTC offset on that date**, not your local clock.
Convert before joining, especially across daylight-saving changes. Open the event
for its full venue, end time, preparation, language, registration, and latest status.
A published event does not establish remaining places or a booking.

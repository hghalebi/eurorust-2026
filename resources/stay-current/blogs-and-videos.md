# Follow the work behind the talks

Use project announcements for release facts, personal writing for technical
arguments, and videos for explanations you can follow at your own pace.

Checked **15 October 2026**. Dates below are the newest items observed in the
inspected feeds. Personal analysis and proposals do not establish stable Rust behavior.

## Find local communities

Use the [Rust community directory](https://rust-lang.org/community/) to find
forums and local groups. For regional announcements, see
[Rust India](https://rustindia.org/) ([RSS](https://rustindia.org/rss.xml)) and
[Rust Africa](https://rustafrica.org/) ([RSS](https://rustafrica.org/feed/)).
An announcement's publication date is separate from its event date.

For books, papers, and a specific next step, return to the
[session reading lists](../related-talks/README.md).

with the question it helps answer.

# Listen to Rust engineers

Choose an engineering question, then an episode. These shows cover production
systems, language design, projects, and embedded work.

Checked **15 October 2026**. Dates below identify the newest item observed in
the inspected feed or publisher listing. They do not predict the next release.
Audio playback was not tested.

# Plan your EuroRust attendance

Use this workflow in an ordinary browser; no agent or build is required.

1. Open [the readable schedule](../exports/schedule.md), note its snapshot, and
   confirm changes in [the official schedule](https://eurorust.eu/schedule/).
2. Write your goals, Rust background, available times, attendance mode, existing
   registrations, and equipment constraints in your own copy of
   [the itinerary template](../templates/itinerary.md).
3. Shortlist sessions. Read their official descriptions and available
   [community summaries](../exports/conference-pack.md). Topic tags are
   interpretations, and missing difficulty/prerequisites mean unknown.
4. Pick one primary session per slot and an alternative. Check workshop tickets,
   setup, prerequisites, venue transfers, lunch, and breaks. Joining a workshop
   already underway depends on the organizer; a link is not a booking.
5. Save your plan and its snapshot. Revisit it when the schedule or your goals
   change. A recording is a fallback when it has actually been published.

[My proposed path](../my-conference/itinerary.md) and
[fictional participant paths](../community/paths/README.md) are examples, not
defaults. Different interests should produce different choices. For a constrained
half-day, omit sessions outside your available interval before ranking topics.

For remote attendance, confirm access for each chosen session: conference-wide
hybrid participation does not establish that a particular workshop is streamed.
Unknown end times prevent a complete overlap check. Leave time for venue changes
using your own travel estimate.

Use [the agent guide](agent-guide.md) if you want help from your own harness.

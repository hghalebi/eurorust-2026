# Record what the next organizer should know

Use actual observations. Identify event/edition, author, role, dates, and what may
be shared.

| Decision or problem | Constraint | Observed outcome | Evidence and limits | Next edition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | |

- What helped people prepare, find sessions, and participate?
- Which setup, schedule, access, or communication issues occurred?
- Which materials were published? What remains unavailable?
- What did feedback cover, and who was missing?
- Which actual costs or attendance figures may be shared? What do they count?
- Who owns follow-up, corrections, and the archive?

Credit people with permission. Separate comments, interpretation, and measured
results. Keep private incidents and participant details in the private process.

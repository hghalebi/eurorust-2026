# Draft a talk or workshop proposal

Copy into your own notes. Follow the event's form and word limits.

- Working title and format:
- Audience and prior knowledge:
- Concrete problem and why it matters:
- What the audience will learn or do:
- Your contribution: experience, evidence, teaching approach, or question:
- Earlier talks and sources; what each covers:
- Abstract: problem, approach, evidence, takeaway:
- Outline and time budget:
- Evidence already observed; experiments still planned:
- Demo or exercise requirements and fallback:
- Relevant speaker biography and public links:
- Access needs, language, and support questions:
- Event/edition, submission URL, deadline, timezone, and check date:
- Recording and material-sharing terms to confirm:

Before submitting, check attribution, unsupported claims, readable slides, the
conduct policy, and planned versus completed work. Keep personal details private.

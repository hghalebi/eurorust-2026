# Propose or record a community activity

Copy this template into an issue or your own notes. Remove prompts you do not
need. Keep unknown details explicit; a proposal is not an announced event.

- **Title:**
- **Status:** proposed / announced / completed / cancelled
- **Format:** talk and experiment / paper or recording club / documentation or
  translation sprint / demo / pairing / topic discussion
- **Audience and goal:**
- **Source:** original talk, paper, project, or page; author and version/date
- **Related session or topic:** optional repository ID and link
- **Host:** confirmed public name or unknown; do not assign someone without consent
- **Date and time:** original wording, timezone, and confirmation source, or unknown
- **Place or remote route:** venue, room, joining page, or unknown
- **Access:** cost, language, captions, accessibility, eligibility, and equipment;
  mark unknowns
- **Preparation:** one useful source and any required experience
- **Small outcome:** a question answered, a note, an example, or a proposed correction
- **Participation rules:** conduct and reporting route; recording and note-sharing consent
- **Opt-in route:** public organizer or project channel, once confirmed

## Questions to explore

1. What do we want to understand?
2. What evidence would help?
3. What can we reasonably finish?

## After the activity

Complete this only from observations. If cancelled, record that instead.

- What happened, when, and in which participation mode?
- What result or artifact can others inspect?
- What failed, remained uncertain, or needs a smaller follow-up?
- Who should receive credit, with their consent?
- What is the next useful step?

Use the [session note template](session.md) for technical observations. Keep
private chats, contact details, attendance lists, credentials, and unlicensed
copies out of public notes.

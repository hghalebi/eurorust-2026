# Research a possible speaker

Use public professional sources. This brief does not establish availability or
permission to contact someone.

- Event, audience, topic, and reason this person's work is relevant:
- Name and identity evidence; namesakes or uncertain matches:
- Original talks, publications, projects, or teaching material:
- What each source supports; date and access limitations:
- Proposed session format and audience benefit:
- Public professional contact route, if the person provides one:
- Support, timing, access, and publication terms your event can offer:
- Questions to resolve before an authorized invitation:

Do not infer willingness to speak, mentor, travel, or work without payment.
Keep private conversations and contact details outside the public repository.

# Plan an event

- Status: proposed / announced / completed / cancelled
- Audience, outcome, format, and scope:
- Organizing owner, public contact, and unassigned roles:
- Dates/timezone, venue/remote route, confirmation sources:
- Program: formats, call, review criteria, conflicts of interest, decisions:
- Speaker support, setup, rehearsal, and publication permissions:
- Access, language, captions, costs, support, and help route:
- Official conduct policy and private reporting channel:

## Delivery and handoff

Record the run sheet, entrances/rooms, equipment checks, fallbacks, change log,
material permissions, and archive owner. Keep private contacts, attendee lists,
and incident details outside public copies.
